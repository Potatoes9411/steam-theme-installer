Give Steam a new look with **Nebula** or **Glassy** — two themes made by **Potatoes9411**.

Pick your favorite, preview its style, and let the installer handle the setup.

## Choose your theme

- **Nebula** — a dark space theme with deep crimson accents.
- **Glassy** — a clean glass-style theme with white and silver accents.

The installer's background and colors change to match your selection.

## What it does

- Downloads and installs your chosen theme.
- Sets up Millennium if you don't already have it. Millennium is what allows Steam to use custom themes.
- Applies the theme automatically.
- Keeps your other themes and plugins in place.
- Lets you start Steam when setup is finished.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- Steam already installed.
- An internet connection.

No extra software needs to be installed beforehand.

## How to install

1. Download **Potatools Theme Installer.exe** from this repository's **Releases** section.
2. Open the installer normally — you don't need to choose **Run as administrator**.
3. Select **Nebula** or **Glassy**, then click **Continue**.
4. Check your Steam folder. Use **Change** if the location is incorrect.
5. Click **Install** and approve the Windows permission prompt.
6. Wait for setup to finish, then click **Start Steam**.

**Save your game before installing.** Setup closes Steam while applying your theme. If Millennium is being installed for the first time, Steam will open briefly and close again during setup. This is expected — keep the installer open until it finishes.

If Steam asks you to sign in or complete an update, finish that step so setup can continue.

## Want to switch themes?

Run the installer again and choose the other theme. It will become your active Steam theme.

## Need help?

If setup runs into a problem, follow the message on screen and click **Retry**. You can also use **Open log** to get details to share when asking for help.
